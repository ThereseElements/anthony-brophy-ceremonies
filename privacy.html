Privacy

How your information is used

When you contact Anthony Brophy Ceremonies by phone, email, WhatsApp or the enquiry form, the details you provide (name, contact details, ceremony date, venue and anything you tell us about your plans) are used only to respond to your enquiry and, if you book, to plan and deliver your ceremony.

Your details are not sold or shared with third parties, except where needed to deliver your ceremony (for example, the Civil Registration Service for a legal marriage) or where required by law.

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and record-keeping obligations, and is then deleted. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you at any time by emailing anthonybrophyceremonies@gmail.com.

This website does not use advertising cookies. The enquiry form is processed by a third-party form service; see the README for the provider in use.
